In 1596, the first edition of Giovanni Antonio Magini's Latin translation of Ptolemy's *Geographia* appeared in print. Leonardo Cernoti translated this edition into Italian, and this was printed in 1597-1598. This 1621 printing of *Geografia cio&#232; Descrittione Universale della Terra* is the second edition of Cernoti's translation. It features 27 Ptolemaic maps and 37 maps of the modern world, including a small folding map of the world, which, as issued, is bound before the section "Discrittione di Tuttol Mondo." The engraved maps were prepared by Girolamo Porro (1520-1604) and are the same as the ones found in the 1597-1598 edition (*Old Decorative Maps and Charts* by Arthur Lee Humphreys). Of the three world maps in the modern section, two are after Rumold Mercator and one is after Abraham Ortelius. The final chapter of the book contains a discussion on America.
