On September 14, 1838, the playwright John Poole accompanied Charles Green on a balloon ascent starting from Vauxhall Gardens at about five minutes past six in the evening. Poole gave an "unscientific" account of this trip in his book *Crotchets in the Air*. The book is presented in the form of a letter to Poole's "dear friend" Tom. Nine passengers took part in the ascent, five of whom were "old ballooners," including Green, his wife Martha Morrell, Mr. Spencer (presumably Edward Spencer), and "two of the Messrs. Hughes." The letter contains some anecdotes and observations during the flight.

The last two pages contain advertisements for Poole's new book along with other works published by Henry Colburn. A second edition of this work was also printed in 1838. The title page of the second edition is the same as in the first edition, with the exception of the words "Second Edition" appearing below the quote by Shakespeare and above the publisher's info.
