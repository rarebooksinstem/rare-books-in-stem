*Unheard of Curiosities* is the English translation of Jacques Gaffarel's book *Curiositez inouyes sur la sculpture talismaniquedes Persans*, first printed in Paris in 1629. The third French edition appeared the same year as this translation (1650). The translator of this work is Edmund Chilmead (1610-1654), who was a translator and writer of music books and history. Gaffarel was a French astrologer who was a student of Arabic and Hebrew texts and "an advocate for enriching and strengthening Christianity by incorporating the wisdom of non-Christian traditions of the Middle East into its doctrines" (*Dignified Retreat: Writers and Intellectuals in the Age of Richeliu* by Robert A. Schneider). More information about Gaffarel can be found on [Linda Hall's website](https://www.lindahall.org/about/news/scientist-of-the-day/jacques-gaffarel/).
